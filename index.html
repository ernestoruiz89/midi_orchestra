⌨️ Controls & Shortcuts Guide

🎮 Free Camera Navigation

  • Left Click + Drag : Orbit / Rotate camera
  • Right Click + Drag : Pan stage
  • Mouse Wheel : Zoom in / Zoom out
  • W A S D / ↑ ← ↓ → : Move freely around the stage
  • Q / E : Lower / Raise camera height
  • Click on Instrument : Focus camera on that instrument

🎵 Music Playback

  • Space : Play / Pause
  • R : Restart song to beginning
  • L : Toggle continuous loop
  • M : Mute / Unmute master sound

✨ Views & Interface

  • 1 - 7 : Quick camera presets
  • C : Toggle Auto Director
  • O : Toggle cinematic orbit
  • V : Toggle dynamic/all instrument visibility
  • H : Open or close this guide
  • Esc : Close open panels
